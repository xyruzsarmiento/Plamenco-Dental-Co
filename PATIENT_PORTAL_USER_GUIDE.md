# Patient Portal User Guide

## Sign in
Use your registered patient account. Your portal should only show records linked to your authenticated account.

## Dashboard
Review upcoming appointments, forms requiring attention, treatment-plan decisions, billing information, recall/follow-up reminders and notifications when available.

## Appointments
Open your own appointment details and use self-service reschedule/cancel only when the clinic has enabled those policies. Real availability and confirmation are required before a change is complete.

## Forms & Consent
Open the exact assigned form version. Signed submissions become read-only historical records. A form is not signed until submission succeeds.

## Treatment Plans
Review the treatment estimate and item decisions. Accepting a treatment plan does not mean it has been paid, completed or automatically scheduled.

## Billing and payments
View real invoices, confirmed payments, balances and receipts. Online payments are only successful after trusted provider/backend confirmation.

## Documents
Only patient-visible documents are available. Private files may use short-lived secure access links.

## Recall & Follow-Up
A reminder may offer a scheduling action. Booking a follow-up does not mean the clinical follow-up has been completed.

## Profile and preferences
Update only the profile and communication preferences enabled by the clinic. Authentication email changes may require a separate confirmation flow.

## PWA / mobile
The portal can provide an installable app shell on supported devices. Sensitive actions such as payments and consent submission require connectivity; they are not silently queued offline.
