# Plamenco Dental Co. Daily Clinic Workflow

## Opening

1. Staff logs in.
2. Select or confirm the working branch: Pulilan, Plaridel, or authorized all-branch view.
3. Open Dashboard and review Today's Patient Flow.
4. Check alerts for pending requests, waiting patients, billing handoffs, inventory alerts, and expense tasks.

## Reception

Use Today's Patient Flow as the primary work surface.

- Search by patient name, patient number, phone, service, or appointment number.
- Use branch filter before performing branch-specific work.
- For scheduled arrivals, use Check In from the row.
- For walk-ins, use Walk-In to open appointment creation, then search/create the patient as needed.
- Do not create a duplicate patient until duplicate warnings have been reviewed.

## Check-In

1. Confirm patient identity.
2. Confirm appointment, branch, service, and dentist.
3. Click Check In.
4. Move patient to Waiting when they are physically ready for the dentist.

Late patients are not automatically cancelled. Staff should follow the clinic's late-arrival policy once approved.

## Dentist Workflow

1. Dentist reviews Dentist Queue.
2. Start Visit for the next assigned/waiting patient.
3. Clinical workspace opens from appointment context.
4. Review allergies, medical conditions, current medications, history, documents, and prior treatments.
5. Record today's notes, treatments, prescriptions, and documents.
6. Finalize the clinical record only when documentation is complete.
7. Complete the appointment when the visit is done.

## Billing

1. Cashier reviews Patients for Billing.
2. Open Billing from the completed visit.
3. Review treatments, charges, invoice totals, discounts, previous balances, and amount due.
4. Record manual payment only once, with method/reference where required.
5. Print receipt where available.
6. Confirm remaining balance if partial payment is accepted.

## Follow-Up

If follow-up is required, schedule it before the patient leaves when possible. Use the patient record and appointment module so the patient does not need to be searched from scratch.

## Inventory

- Record stock received to the correct branch.
- Stock movements must affect only the intended branch.
- Review low-stock alerts before end of day.
- Do not adjust inventory without an operational reason.

## Expenses

- Record expenses against the correct branch.
- Use configured payment methods.
- Attach or retain proof according to clinic policy.
- Review overdue and due-soon expenses during daily close.

## End Of Day

1. Confirm all checked-in or in-treatment appointments are completed, cancelled, or otherwise explained.
2. Review unpaid/partially paid invoices.
3. Reconcile cashier collections by branch and payment method.
4. Review no-shows and reschedule follow-ups.
5. Review inventory and expense exceptions.
6. Super Admin/Admin reviews branch performance and unresolved blockers.

## Outage Rule

If database/network errors occur, staff must not treat the action as saved unless the system confirms it. Follow the business continuity and incident response guides.
