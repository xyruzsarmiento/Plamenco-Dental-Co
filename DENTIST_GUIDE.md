# Plamenco Dental Co. Dentist Guide

Use your own clinic account so clinical actions are attributable.

1. Log in and review Dashboard or today's schedule.
2. Open waiting patients from the queue or appointment list.
3. Review patient profile, clinical history, dental chart, documents, allergies, and medical conditions.
4. Start visit only for the correct patient and appointment.
5. Record clinical notes clearly.
6. Add treatments with provider, branch, service, tooth/area when relevant, and price snapshot.
7. Create prescriptions when needed.
8. Complete visit when treatment documentation is finished.
9. Set follow-up instructions or next appointment recommendation.
10. Avoid deleting finalized clinical history; use amendment/void workflows where available.
