# Plamenco Dental Co. Patient Guide

1. Register with your real email and contact details.
2. Confirm email if required.
3. Log in to the patient portal.
4. Use Forgot Password if you cannot access your account.
5. Review and update profile details when allowed.
6. Book appointments by selecting branch, service, date, and available slot.
7. Review appointment history and status updates.
8. Check notifications for clinic messages.
9. Review billing, payments, and receipts when available.
10. Contact the clinic if any record looks incorrect.
