# Staff Quick Start

## Check In A Scheduled Patient

1. Open Dashboard.
2. Search today's patient or appointment.
3. Confirm branch, time, service, and dentist.
4. Click Check In.
5. Click Waiting when the patient is ready to be seen.

## Add A Walk-In

1. Open Dashboard.
2. Click Walk-In.
3. Search the patient first by name, patient number, or phone.
4. If no match exists, create the patient without requiring portal registration.
5. Create the walk-in appointment with branch, service/reason, and dentist.
6. Check in the patient.

## Mark No Show

1. Open Appointments or Today's Patient Flow.
2. Confirm the patient has not arrived and the clinic no-show rule applies.
3. Use Mark No Show.
4. Enter the reason when prompted.

## Record Payment

1. Open Dashboard.
2. Review Patients for Billing.
3. Open Billing for the patient.
4. Check invoice items, discounts, prior balance, and amount due.
5. Record payment with method and reference if required.
6. Print receipt when available.

## End Of Day

- No active patient should remain stuck in Checked In, Waiting, or In Treatment.
- Review unpaid invoices and partial payments.
- Reconcile collections by branch and payment method.
- Review no-shows, inventory alerts, and expense tasks.
- Escalate sync, payment, or branch mismatches before closing.
