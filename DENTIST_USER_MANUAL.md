# Dentist User Manual

## Today and schedule
Use the dentist workspace for your assigned schedule and authorized patient context. Appointment status and branch/provider assignment remain separate from the clinical record lifecycle.

## Medical history
Review the patient's recorded medical history and intake provenance. 'No allergies recorded' is different from an explicit patient confirmation of no allergies.

## Clinical records
Create or edit drafts through the clinical workflow. Finalize only when the record is ready. Finalized records must not be destructively edited; use the amendment workflow when permitted.

## Treatments
Record treatment as performed only when it actually occurred. Planned/recommended treatment must not appear as completed treatment or billing solely because it exists in a plan.

## Treatment plans
Use quoted historical price snapshots. Acceptance is a patient decision about a plan, not payment, treatment completion or an appointment. Scheduling must use the appointment engine.

## Prescriptions and documents
Use existing authorized prescription/document workflows. Keep internal clinical notes and restricted files out of patient-visible channels unless specifically configured for patient access.

## Follow-up
A follow-up recommendation is not an appointment. Use the follow-up/recall workflow to record the due context and let scheduling create a real appointment.
