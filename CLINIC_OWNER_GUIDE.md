# Plamenco Dental Co. Clinic Owner Guide

Use owner/report access to review business performance without changing operational records unnecessarily.

## Daily Review

- Dashboard totals and alerts.
- Pulilan and Plaridel appointment volume.
- Collections, outstanding balances, and payment discrepancies.
- No-shows, cancellations, and pending requests.
- Low stock and critical supplies.

## Weekly Review

- Branch performance.
- Provider activity.
- Revenue and collections.
- Expenses and net operating result.
- Inventory movement and discrepancy trends.
- Report exports for accounting review.

## Controls

- Confirm who may view/export financial reports.
- Confirm discount and refund authority.
- Require audit review for account changes, payment adjustments, data imports, and configuration changes.
