# Front Desk User Manual

## Start of shift
Sign in with your own account, confirm the active branch and open the dashboard/today workspace.

## Patient handling
Search for an existing patient before creating a new record. Confirm identity and contact details before changing patient information.

## Appointments
Use the appointment engine for booking, rescheduling and cancellation. Only select real available slots. Record check-in, queue and no-show states only when they actually occur. Do not manually treat a cancelled or no-show appointment as completed.

## Forms and intake
Check the patient's intake and required-form status before the visit. A pending consent task does not mean the form is signed; use the real Forms & Consent workflow.

## Billing handoff
Open the patient's real invoice/payment workflow. Do not mark a payment successful based on a screenshot or browser redirect alone when provider verification is required.

## Recall and follow-up
Use the Recall & Follow-Up workspace for actual contact attempts, booking and rescheduling outcomes. Booking a recall does not mean treatment is complete.

## Tasks
Use Tasks / Work Queue to claim or update work you are authorized to handle. Closing a task must not substitute for completing the source workflow.

## Errors
If a page fails to load, retry or report the error. Do not interpret a failed query as 'no records'.
