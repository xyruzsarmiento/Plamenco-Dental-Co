# Plamenco Dental Co. Business Metrics Definitions

Part 33 reporting contract. These definitions are intentionally conservative: dashboards must derive values from operational records, preserve branch/date context, and never substitute a convenient timestamp or current catalog price for the business event being reported.

## Global reporting rules

- Business timezone: `Asia/Manila`.
- `All Branches` means the appropriate aggregate of Pulilan + Plaridel plus explicitly labelled clinic-wide/unmapped records where the source permits them. Unknown historical branch values are never assigned to Pulilan.
- Query failure is an error state, not a zero-value state.
- Imported historical records use their real business dates where available; `imported_at`/import execution time is not a patient-acquisition or treatment date.
- Aggregations must deduplicate entities before joining one-to-many relationships. Never multiply invoice totals by invoice items/payments or treatment totals by service joins.
- Financial reports are management/operational reports, not formal accounting statements.

| Metric | Definition | Source / date basis | Branch behavior | Included / excluded | Calculation / limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Collections | Successfully recorded incoming patient payments in the selected period. | Trusted payments/payment allocations; payment/effective date. | Payment/invoice branch according to billing source of truth. | Include successful/posted payments. Exclude failed, pending gateway attempts, cancelled/voided payments and duplicate webhook attempts. | Sum successful incoming payments. Never use invoices as a proxy. |
| Gross Collections | Successful incoming payments before refunds. | Payments; payment date. | Same as Collections. | Same successful-payment rules. Refunds excluded from this number and shown separately. | Sum successful incoming payment amount. |
| Refunds | Valid completed refunds in the selected period. | Refund/payment records; refund effective date. | Follow original transaction/branch relationship. | Completed/posted refunds only. | Report separately so gross and net remain explainable. |
| Net Collections | Gross Collections less valid completed refunds, only where refund architecture supports reliable attribution. | Payments + refunds; their effective business dates. | Same trusted branch attribution as source records. | Successful payments and completed refunds only. | Gross Collections - Refunds. Do not call this accounting revenue. |
| Billed Amount | Value of valid patient invoices/charges issued for the selected period. | Invoice/billing source; invoice/business issue date. | Invoice/visit branch. | Include valid issued/open/paid invoices according to actual statuses. Exclude cancelled/void invoices. | Sum stored historical invoice/charge values; never recalculate from today's service price. |
| Outstanding Receivables | Legitimate unpaid patient balances as of the reporting context. | Invoice balances/payment allocations; invoice/due date for aging. | Invoice branch; unmapped remains labelled. | Valid non-void invoices less successfully applied payments/credits according to billing architecture. | Use billing source-of-truth balance/allocation logic; avoid join multiplication. |
| Recorded Expenses | Valid clinic expense records for the selected period. | Expense architecture; expense/business date. | Pulilan/Plaridel stay separate; clinic-wide remains clinic-wide under All Branches. | Valid recorded expenses. Exclude cancelled/voided records where statuses exist. | Sum expense source of truth. Inventory purchases linked to an expense are counted once, not again from PO total. |
| Collections Less Recorded Expenses | Management cash movement indicator, not profit. | Collections and Recorded Expenses under identical filters. | Same filter context for both inputs. | Uses the definitions above. | Collections - Recorded Expenses. Never label `Net Profit`. |
| Appointments | Appointment records whose scheduled/start date falls in the selected period. | Appointments; scheduled/start date. | Appointment branch. | Status distribution uses actual appointment statuses. | Count distinct appointments; do not aggregate by `created_at`. |
| Completed Visits | Appointments/visits explicitly completed and eligible to have occurred. | Appointment/visit; scheduled/start/completion business date per report. | Visit branch. | Completed status only; future appointments excluded. | Count distinct completed visits. |
| No Shows | Appointments explicitly marked no-show. | Appointments; scheduled/start date. | Appointment branch. | `no_show` status only (or canonical equivalent). Cancelled and future appointments excluded. | Count distinct no-show appointments. |
| No-Show Rate | Share of eligible occurred appointments marked no-show. | Appointments; scheduled/start date. | Appointment branch. | Numerator: No Shows. Denominator: eligible scheduled appointments that have reached their scheduled time; exclude cancelled and future appointments. | No Shows / eligible occurred appointments. Final clinic policy remains a business decision. |
| Cancellation Rate | Share of relevant appointments cancelled under the documented report context. | Appointments; scheduled/start date. | Appointment branch. | Cancelled status in numerator; denominator must be labelled in UI/report. | Do not silently mix future appointments into an occurrence-based rate. |
| Completion Rate | Completed visits divided by appointments eligible to have occurred. | Appointments/visits; scheduled/start date. | Appointment branch. | Completed in numerator; exclude future appointments from denominator and do not blindly include cancelled appointments. | Formula must be displayed/documented with report. |
| New Patients | Patients whose reliable first clinic visit/business acquisition date falls in period. | Patient/visit history; first reliable clinic visit date, not import timestamp. | First known visit branch where reliable; unknown stays unmapped. | Historical imports count only when a genuine business date supports the classification. | Never count an Excel import batch as new patients on import day. |
| Returning Patients | Patients seen in period with a reliable prior visit before the selected visit/period. | Visit history; visit date. | Visit branch. | Requires reliable historical visit history. | If history is incomplete, surface limitation rather than infer. |
| Patients Seen | Distinct patients attached to eligible occurred/completed visits in period. | Visits/appointments; visit/scheduled date. | Visit branch. | Use report-specific eligible statuses. | Count distinct patient IDs, not appointment rows when multiple rows can represent one visit. |
| Service Count | Number of actual performed treatment/service records in period. | Treatments/clinical service records; treatment/visit date. | Treatment/visit branch. | Performed/finalized records according to clinical architecture. | Rank using actual historical activity, not current service catalog price. |
| Service Billed Value | Stored historical billed/charge value attributable to performed services. | Treatment/invoice snapshots; treatment/invoice business date. | Underlying visit/invoice branch. | Valid historical records only. | Never recalculate old treatment values using current service prices. |
| Provider Activity | Neutral provider operational metrics such as patients seen, visits, treatments and billed value. | Appointments/visits/treatments; their business dates. | Actual provider branch assignment on the activity. | Completed/performed records as appropriate. | Do not label providers best/worst; workloads, schedules and case mix differ. |
| Provider Utilization | Booked clinical minutes divided by actual available scheduled clinical minutes. | Provider schedules + appointments; schedule/appointment date. | Provider's actual branch schedule. | Only reliable schedule windows and eligible bookings. | Do not display if denominator cannot be derived reliably; never assume 8 hours/day. |
| Inventory Value | Management inventory valuation only when reliable unit-cost data exists. | Branch stock + reliable unit cost; point-in-time. | Pulilan and Plaridel quantities remain separate; aggregate must be labelled. | Valid on-hand stock with reliable cost basis. | Use documented cost basis only; do not invent FIFO/LIFO. Hide metric if cost basis is unreliable. |
| Low Stock Items | Branch stock at/below configured reorder threshold. | Inventory branch stock; current point-in-time. | Evaluated independently per branch. | Active inventory items with configured reorder threshold. | Never use an arbitrary hard-coded threshold. |
| Cash Variance | Difference between actual and expected physical closing cash for a cash session. | Cash sessions; session/closing date. | Sessions remain branch-specific. | Physical cash only. Exclude GCash, card, online gateway and bank transfer from expected physical cash. | Actual Closing Cash - Expected Closing Cash (sign should be clearly labelled). Non-zero variance is an operational exception. |

## Drill-down contract

Every executive KPI must preserve the active date range and branch when opening its underlying report. Where applicable, it must also preserve provider, service, status, payment method, expense category or inventory category. The detailed report total must reconcile to the originating KPI.

## Sensitive metrics

Provider compensation, clinic-wide financial analytics, cash reconciliation and sensitive patient drill-downs are restricted by existing RBAC/RLS. Frontend filter selection is never authorization.
