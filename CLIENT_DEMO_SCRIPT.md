# Plamenco Dental Co. Client Demo Script

Use a staging environment with approved sample data. Do not create fake production patients or transactions.

1. Open landing page, login page, register page, and forgot-password page.
2. Show Super Admin dashboard, health checks, clinic configuration, roles, permissions, account invitations, and audit logs.
3. Review Pulilan and Plaridel branch configuration, schedules, providers, and service visibility.
4. Demonstrate patient search, duplicate warnings, patient profile, notes, and account status handling.
5. Create a safe appointment request, review it as staff, assign provider, confirm, check in, and move through queue.
6. Open the visit as dentist, review clinical history, add notes/treatment/prescription, and complete visit.
7. Create invoice, record manual payment, generate receipt, and explain online payment verification flow.
8. Show inventory stock, purchase receiving, branch transfer, adjustment, and low-stock reporting.
9. Record an operating expense and review expense payment/attachment handling.
10. Open reports, filter by date/branch/provider/service, and export PDF/Excel/CSV only as an authorized role.
11. Open data import workspace and explain upload, mapping, validation, duplicate review, dry run, import, reconciliation, and rollback limits.
12. Close with launch blockers, clinic decisions, backup/restore plan, and sign-off checklist.
