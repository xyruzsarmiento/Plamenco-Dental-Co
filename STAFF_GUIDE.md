# Plamenco Dental Co. Staff Guide

Use clinic-issued accounts only. Do not share credentials.

## Daily Workflow

1. Log in and open Dashboard.
2. Review today's appointments, pending requests, queue, payment alerts, and low-stock notices.
3. Search patients before creating a new patient.
4. For walk-ins, reuse an existing patient when a duplicate is found.
5. Book or confirm appointments, assign dentist, and check branch/date/time carefully.
6. Use Calendar to review daily schedule.
7. Check in arriving patients and move them through Queue statuses.
8. Record manual payments only after confirming invoice, payer, amount, method, and reference.
9. Generate receipts after successful payment.
10. Mark no-shows and reschedules according to clinic policy.
11. Record inventory stock in/out, transfers, and counts; use adjustments for corrections.
12. Enter expenses with category, branch/scope, amount, date, vendor, and receipt path where applicable.
13. Run reports only if your role allows it.
